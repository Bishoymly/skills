Workspace billing

Give workspace owners a clear, recoverable grace period after a failed renewal.

The change records a short grace window, keeps the workspace usable while payment can be fixed, and makes the state visible in the billing experience. It introduces one small billing workflow across the database, API, UI, and scheduled enforcement.

At a glance

Failed renewals no longer immediately restrict a workspace. A billing grace period starts after a retryable payment failure, ends when payment succeeds or the window expires, and is surfaced where an owner can act on it.
